# CricSim — v12 Alpha 1.4 (PC playtest)

CricSim is an offline cricket simulator for desktop browsers. After months of development, this is a public **alpha playtest**: the goal is to discover what works, what needs balancing, and what needs fixing.

## Download and play

**[Download CricSim v12 Alpha 1.4 — Playtester ZIP](./CricSim_v12_Alpha1_4_Playtester.zip)**

1. Download and **extract** the ZIP to your PC (do not launch the HTML from inside the ZIP).
2. Open `CricSim_v12_ALPHA1_4_Portable_Save_Folder.html` in the latest **Chrome or Edge**.
3. Start a Quick Match or create an IPL-style tournament. Internet access is not needed for normal play.
4. Read `START_HERE_PLAYTESTERS.md` for the full guide, especially how to **back up your universe**.

**This is an alpha, not a certified stable release. Keep backups of your data.**

## What's in the game?

- Formats including T20, ODI, Test, first-class, T10 and The Hundred.
- Full IPL-style tournament seasons, squad and player management, and custom competitions.
- Commentary, scorecards, statistics and tournament standings.
- Optional DRS, weather/rain and pitch conditions.
- P12E batter retirement and limited injury rules in standard full-XI matches and competitions.
- **Portable Save Folder**: save full-universe snapshots and restore on another computer. Use **Save Now** and **Verify Folder** before transfer. Browser folder permissions may need renewal.
- PlanetCricket forum post exporting. The **plain BBCode fallback** works without custom site tags; enhanced styling awaits forum-side support.

## What we'd love you to test

- Complete an IPL season and inspect results, Orange/Purple Cap leaders, playoff progression and low-rated breakout performers.
- Try unusual squads, scores, conditions, bowling combinations and a few different formats.
- Test save/reopen/restore: please make a **separate backup** and avoid overwriting valuable saves.
- If something looks wrong, tell us the player ratings and match scorecard, not just that it feels unrealistic.
